# Review Knowledge Bundle — DEMO ONLY

This synthetic bundle shows how a reviewer or CI job can restore an entire
review knowledge folder through multicloud-py. These examples are fictional;
they were not extracted from real reviews and are not repository policy.

## DEMO-001: Verify uploaded content

After uploading a review artifact, download it and compare the full content
before reporting success. Preserve local artifacts when verification fails.

## DEMO-002: Preserve source context

Keep reusable guidance together with structured rules and source metadata so
another reviewer can inspect the context. Real learning requires confirmed
human authorship; the comments in this demo are synthetic examples only.
